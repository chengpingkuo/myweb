How to compare them

There's no scoreboard — the fun is in the differences. Three passes are enough to see what each AI did with the same ride.

1

Start with the original

Open myTravel first and get the real tour in your head — the route, the photos, the order of the days. Everything else is a retelling of this.

2

Open two editions side by side

Pick any pair — Gemini vs. Claude is a good start. Look at what each one leads with: the biggest photo, the first headline, and what it chose to leave out entirely.

3

Pick your favourite storyteller

Work through all six (the counter at the top of the editions list keeps track as you go) and decide which AI you'd trust to tell your next ride. Then go ride it.
